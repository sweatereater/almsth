## Точный финальный набор промптов

Для каждого PNG полный промпт собран строго как: общий префикс ниже + один перевод строки (`\n`) + соответствующий суффикс. Префикс и пять суффиксов приведены дословно из успешных финальных вызовов, без реконструкции и последующего редактирования.

### Общий префикс

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original full-body fantasy game character concept illustration, one friendly animated skeleton adventurer standing normally on the ground.
Input images: Image 1 is a style reference for jagged hand-drawn black ink, ivory skull shapes and magenta graphic accents. Image 2 is a style reference for handmade bone-punk clothing, scratchy crosshatching and bold round pink glass. These are artistic style references only; make an original character, not a copy.
Medium: sophisticated underground comic art, crisp angular black shapes, selective crosshatching, distressed screen-print on warm off-white paper. Bone ivory, black and charcoal, with raspberry-magenta and dusty teal accents.
Framing: portrait 2:3 illustration, a single complete figure with the whole head and both feet visible, generous clear margins, eye-level slight three-quarter view. Character fills 85% of the height. Simple abstract brush blocks on off-white paper; no narrative scene. Focus on a strong readable silhouette and worn tailored clothing. Natural adult proportions, not chibi. No text, lettering, logo, watermark, UI, inset portrait or collage. Nonviolent fantasy character design.
```
