# Prototype art prompts

All five raster assets were produced with the built-in ImageGen tool. They use
calm painterly classical romantic fantasy, stylized realism, muted stone and
bone colors, no text, UI, logos or watermarks.

## `camp-base.png`

Wide 16:9 prototype base background: a cramped forgotten underground refuge,
seen from the side at a slight elevated three-quarter angle. Rough old dungeon
stonework surrounds a safe alcove. A square ceiling hatch casts dusty light on
an empty landing area; a modest bone hearth and scavenged bedding show only the
very beginning of a camp. Quiet, safe and melancholic, with cold shadows and a
small warm amber glow. No character or established settlement.

## `intro-01-wandering.png`

Wide 16:9 first story still: exactly one complete, unarmed and unclothed
skeleton wanders alone through an ancient stone corridor. Full readable figure,
cautious uncertain posture, cold dim light, dust and restrained detail. Lonely
and introspective rather than horrific.

## `intro-02-poncho.png`

Edit of the first still: preserve skeleton identity, dungeon, camera and
composition. Add only a simple worn dusty brown-gray travel poncho and a small
hand gesture as the skeleton curiously adjusts its first piece of clothing.

## `intro-03-awakening.png`

Edit of the poncho still: preserve identity, clothing, dungeon and composition.
A restrained turquoise soul-light glows inside the rib cage and faintly in the
eyes; the skeleton stands straighter and looks at its chest. Mysterious and
intimate, with no flesh, full transformation or magical explosion.

## `death-bones.png`

Edit of the camp still: preserve the refuge, hatch, hearth, bedding and camera.
Add a non-gory loose heap of recognizable skeleton bones directly beneath the
hatch, a torn poncho scrap and faint settling dust. Melancholy and slightly
darkly humorous, with no blood, flesh or other characters.

## Expanded camp service set

The following four assets were produced with the built-in ImageGen tool for the expanded
camp and its interactive service objects. The shared direction was realistic painterly dark
fantasy, old stone and weathered iron/wood, readable silhouettes, no text, logos or UI.

### `camp-base-expanded.png`

Wide empty underground refuge viewed from the side at a slight elevated angle. Preserve the
ceiling hatch and a quiet safe mood, but remove the initial fire and established furnishings;
show materially more free stone floor for future camp objects and keep the edges dark enough
for interface framing.

### `camp-crusher.png`

Isolated primitive dungeon crusher on transparency: a compact stone-and-rusted-iron press with
a long wrapped lever, heavy jaws and a stable masonry base. Match the camp perspective and
lighting; no operator, floor, labels or surrounding room.

### `camp-whetstone.png`

Isolated foot-powered whetstone on transparency: a large worn grinding wheel in a rough wood
and rusted-iron frame with crank, pedal and tool rest. Match the camp perspective and restrained
lighting; no operator, floor, sparks, labels or surrounding room.

### `death-bones-overlay.png`

Isolated non-gory heap of recognizable skeleton bones on transparency, including a readable
skull and scattered ribs/limbs, intended to land beneath the same expanded camp hatch. Warm
dusty rim light only; no floor, blood, flesh, text or other characters.

## Ritual table and campfire

Both assets below were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, using
`camp-base-expanded.png`, `camp-crusher.png` and `camp-whetstone.png` as local visual
references. They are independent transparent overlays and do not replace the base image.

### `camp-ritual-table.png`

Single isolated compact ancient ritual binding table/altar, side-on three-quarter and slightly
top-down. Handmade dark wood, rough dungeon stone, iron bands, a few bones and torn cloth,
with a shallow circular soul-binding groove and restrained pale turquoise-violet rune glow.
Calm dark realistic fantasy, no character, weapon, gore, text, room, floor or wall; genuine
transparent alpha outside the object and between its legs; upper-left camp lighting.

### `camp-campfire.png`

Single isolated small newly built campfire in a low rough stone ring with three dark pieces of
firewood, modest warm orange flames and a few embers, seen from the same three-quarter
slightly top-down camp perspective. Compact optional room object, no character, cooking pot,
furniture, text, room, floor or wall; tightly contained glow and genuine transparent alpha.
